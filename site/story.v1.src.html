The biology

A synapse that should not exist

Over the last decade, experiments in mice and patient-derived tumours showed that neurons form working synapses onto glioma cells, and that this input makes tumours grow. Five genes carry the story. NeuroBridge treats them as hypotheses to test, not as biomarkers.

Glutamate is released

The presynaptic terminal packs glutamate into vesicles with the transporter SLC17A7 (VGLUT1). When the neuron fires, vesicles fuse and glutamate crosses the cleft.

Venkataramani et al., Nature 2019 · PMID 31534219

AMPA receptors on the tumour cell

Glioma cells express AMPA receptors (GRIA1, GRIA2). Glutamate opens them and depolarises the tumour cell, which drives invasion and growth.

Venkatesh et al., Nature 2019 · PMID 31534222

NMDA signalling

GRIN2B, an NMDA receptor subunit, adds a calcium-permeable route. NMDA signalling at neuron–tumour contacts promotes growth of brain tumours.

Zeng et al., Nature 2019 · PMID 31534217

Neuroligin-3 is shed

Activity cleaves NLGN3 from neurons and glia. The soluble fragment acts as a mitogen on glioma cells; without it, xenografts barely grow.

Venkatesh et al., Cell 2015 · PMID 25913192

BDNF strengthens the synapse

BDNF binding its receptor TrkB (NTRK2) recruits more AMPA receptors, the same plasticity that underlies learning.

Taylor et al., Nature 2023 · PMID 37914930

The platform

Seven agents, one audit trail

Each agent calls typed tools from a registry. Every call is logged with its inputs, outputs and limitations. Claims are written from computed results with fixed templates, then audited by a reviewer that knows the difference between an association and a cause.

01Hypothesis

Splits the question into tests with explicit nulls.

02Literature

Pulls verified records from Europe PMC by PMID.

03Data

Loads TCGA, CGGA, GEO and Visium; hashes every file.

04Bioinformatics

Scores, subtypes, differential expression, cell types, tissue.

05Statistics

Cox models, assumption checks, confounding.

06Validation

Replication in independent cohorts only.

07Reviewer

Grades claims, flags causal language and gaps.

How far the evidence reaches

The answer, graded

The platform states the ceiling of its own design. Observational transcriptomes can show an association. They cannot show a mechanism or a cause. That decision is useful in itself: bulk RNA signatures are the wrong readout for neuron–glioma coupling, and spatial or functional readouts are where a programme should go next.

Causal

Out of reach for this design: no perturbation, randomisation or longitudinal data.

Mechanistic

From the cited experiments only (mouse and xenograft models), never claimed from these data.

Association · reached

The Proneural–Mesenchymal difference replicates in two independent cohorts, but not once normal-brain content is controlled for. Survival does not replicate.

Plausibility

Receptor transcripts spread through tumour tissue in one section: a lead for spatial and functional follow-up.
